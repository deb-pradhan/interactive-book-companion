REMEMBER THIS 🎯

You do not rise to your goals. You fall to your systems.

Goals set the direction, but systems make the progress. Forget the finish line for a minute and fix the daily routine that quietly gets you there. Small habits do not add up. They compound.

Habit stacking

After I [current habit], I will [new habit]. Anchor the new thing to something you already do without thinking.

⏱️

The two-minute rule

Scale any habit down until it takes two minutes. "Read before bed" becomes "read one page." Show up first, optimize later.

Identity over outcomes

Every action is a vote for the type of person you want to become. You do not "run a marathon," you "become a runner," and each run casts a ballot.

🧲

Temptation bundling

Pair something you want to do with something you need to do. Only watch your show while on the exercise bike. The craving carries the chore.

🪞

Environment beats willpower

Make the good habit obvious and the bad one invisible. You do not need more discipline if the chips are out of the house.

📉

The plateau of latent potential

Results lag effort. You feel stuck in the "valley of disappointment" right before the breakthrough. Habits often need to cross a threshold before they pay out.
